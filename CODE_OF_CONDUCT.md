# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors regardless of experience, identity or background.
English and Chinese are both welcome. Critique ideas and code with evidence,
respect different perspectives, and help newcomers understand project decisions.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention,
spam and deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable in project
Issues, Discussions, pull requests and other spaces maintained by this project.
Disagreement and a maintainer declining a change are not misconduct by themselves.

Report concerns privately through the intake in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Do not include sensitive allegations in a public thread. Maintainers will assess
reports in context, protect privacy as far as practical, and may request a
correction, warn a participant, lock/remove content or restrict participation.
Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban. When safe and appropriate,
maintainers will explain the decision and consider an appeal through private
intake. A maintainer involved in a report should recuse themselves where another
maintainer is available; this small project cannot promise independent mediation.

Maintainers must apply these expectations to their own conduct as well. This
policy is limited to project spaces and official representation of the project.
